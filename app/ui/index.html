What this device can show

Charging test

Reads charging power once a second for 30 seconds and looks for drops. Plug in the charger and cable you want to check. Keep the battery below 80% for the clearest result.

Port watch

Watches every port for 10 seconds and flags a cable that keeps dropping and reconnecting. Plug and unplug while it runs if you're testing a loose connection.

Both ends

Checks each plug of a cable in both positions. Results that disagree can point to one faulty end. One failing position can point to a dirty contact.

Drive speed

Writes a 256 MB temporary file to a folder you pick on a drive, times it, then deletes it. Nothing else on the drive is touched.

What we found

    Where do these results come from?

    Reported: the hardware said so. Measured: QloudiCable timed or metered it. Worked out: calculated from the USB specification.

    Words used here
    E-marker
    A small chip inside some cables that states what the cable can carry.
    Charger offers
    The voltage and power levels a charger says it can supply.
    Contract
    The voltage and current the charger and device agreed on.

    Tools

    The self-check tests that this report is internally consistent. It does not test any cable.
